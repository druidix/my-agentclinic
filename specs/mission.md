# Mission

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans.

## Who it serves

- **Agents (primary):** browse ailments, find therapies, and book appointments.
- **Clinic staff:** use a dashboard to see agents, ailments, therapies, and the appointment schedule.

## Target audience

- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.

## Principles

- **Reliable:** a boring, popular, well-understood stack (Mary, engineering).
- **Useful features:** agents, ailments, therapies, and appointment booking (Susan, product).
- **Attractive:** a polished site that works well in modern browsers (Steve, marketing).
